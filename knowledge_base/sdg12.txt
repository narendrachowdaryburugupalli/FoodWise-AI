FOODWISE AI — SDG 12 KNOWLEDGE

UN SDG 12 focuses on Responsible Consumption and Production.

FoodWise AI is aligned with SDG 12 because the system is designed to support more efficient food preparation and reduce avoidable food waste in campuses and institutions.

Target 12.3 focuses on reducing food waste and food losses by 2030.

FoodWise AI contributes to this objective through:
- Food demand forecasting
- Historical food-waste analysis
- Surplus detection
- Preparation recommendations
- Redistribution planning
- Sustainability impact tracking

The system is a decision-support prototype. It does not claim that all food waste can be eliminated.

FoodWise AI should be evaluated using measurable indicators such as:
- Meals prepared
- Meals consumed
- Meals wasted
- Waste percentage
- Forecast accuracy
- Potential surplus
- Food redistributed

Impact values shown by the prototype may use illustrative assumptions and should not be presented as measured real-world savings unless supported by actual data.
