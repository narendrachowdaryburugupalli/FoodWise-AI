FOODWISE AI — FOOD REDISTRIBUTION KNOWLEDGE

Food redistribution is a potential response when safe, edible food is expected to remain after meal service.

FoodWise AI can identify potential surplus and provide sample redistribution planning.

A redistribution workflow can include:

1. Detect potential surplus.
2. Estimate the quantity available.
3. Check whether the food is safe and suitable for redistribution.
4. Check approved redistribution partners.
5. Check partner capacity and availability.
6. Allocate food according to approved procedures.
7. Record the quantity redistributed.
8. Record the outcome for impact measurement.

Food safety must always take priority.

The FoodWise AI prototype does not independently certify food as safe for redistribution.

The prototype's sample partner records are demonstration data only. They do not represent confirmed NGO availability, real-time capacity or actual partnership agreements.

Final redistribution decisions should be made by authorized institutional staff according to applicable food-safety procedures and local requirements.

Potential redistribution indicators include:
- Surplus meals identified
- Meals approved for redistribution
- Meals actually redistributed
- Partner capacity
- Redistribution completion rate
