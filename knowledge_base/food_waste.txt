FOODWISE AI — FOOD WASTE REDUCTION KNOWLEDGE

Food waste can occur when more food is prepared than is consumed.

In a campus or institutional dining environment, food demand can vary because of:
- Number of students
- Events
- Special menu days
- Day of the week
- Menu type
- Attendance patterns
- Historical consumption patterns

FoodWise AI uses these types of inputs to estimate expected food consumption.

Basic surplus calculation:

Potential Surplus = Food Prepared - Predicted Demand

If potential surplus is positive, the system identifies a possible surplus.

Example:

Food Prepared = 150 meals
Predicted Demand = 130 meals

Potential Surplus = 150 - 130 = 20 meals

The system can classify potential surplus risk using demonstration thresholds:

More than 25 meals surplus:
HIGH risk

More than 10 and up to 25 meals surplus:
MEDIUM risk

More than 0 and up to 10 meals surplus:
LOW risk

Zero or negative surplus:
NO SURPLUS

These thresholds are prototype rules and should be validated using real institutional data before operational deployment.

Food preparation recommendations should not blindly follow the model. Kitchen staff should consider operational requirements, attendance uncertainty, food safety and other local constraints.
