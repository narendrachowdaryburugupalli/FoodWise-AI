FOODWISE AI — RESPONSIBLE AI KNOWLEDGE

Responsible AI is an important part of FoodWise AI.

The system should provide transparent and human-supervised decision support.

TRANSPARENCY

Users should understand:
- What information is used by the prediction model.
- What the model predicts.
- How surplus is calculated.
- What assumptions are used.
- What limitations exist.

FAIRNESS

FoodWise AI should avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information.

The system should focus on aggregated operational information such as:
- Student counts
- Menu information
- Events
- Historical consumption
- Food preparation quantities

PRIVACY

Individual student information should not be collected unless it is necessary, authorized and appropriately protected.

Where possible, aggregated information should be used instead of personally identifiable information.

HUMAN OVERSIGHT

FoodWise AI should not independently make final decisions about:
- Food safety
- Food quality
- Redistribution eligibility
- Beneficiary eligibility
- Final kitchen preparation quantities

Authorized staff should review important recommendations before taking action.

LIMITATIONS

Model predictions can be inaccurate.

Historical patterns may not represent future behavior.

Changes in attendance, events, menus, holidays or unexpected circumstances can affect demand.

Impact calculations based on assumptions should be clearly identified as estimates.

FoodWise AI should be treated as decision-support software rather than an autonomous decision-maker.
